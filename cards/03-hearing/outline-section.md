# CARD 03-OUTLINE: Hearing outline section (one issue)
Tier: LOCAL_REVIEW | Output: Markdown | Temperature: 0.1

## Your one job
Write the hearing outline section for ONE issue from its issue sheet.

## You receive
- The issue as stated in the petition, the attorney-supplied standard (or none), and the issue sheet.

## Format
```
## Issue: [as stated in petition]
Standard: [attorney-supplied text, or STANDARD NEEDED]
### Our key facts
- [fact] (cite; ID)
### Deposition designations
- [Witness] [page:line]: "[quote]" (read verbatim / reference)
### Defense position
- [what the defense report or filing says] (cite; ID)
### Our response points
- [defense point] -> [our fact] (cite; ID)
### Open items
- [missing records, S facts, conflicts]
```

## Do not
- No legal standard unless the attorney supplied it. No quote that is not word for word.
- Never write DME or IME.

## Final check
- Every line cited. Conflicts listed, not resolved.
