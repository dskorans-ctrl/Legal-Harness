# CARD 03-CLOSING: Closing outline (one issue)
Tier: STRONG (stronger model or attorney) | Output: Markdown | Temperature: 0.2

## Your one job
Write the closing points for ONE issue, from the reviewed outline section.

## Format
```
## [Issue]
Standard: [attorney-supplied or STANDARD NEEDED]
1. [our strongest fact] (cite)
2. [next fact] (cite)
Defense says: [their main point] (cite)
Our answer: [fact that answers it] (cite)
Relief requested: [exactly as the attorney stated]
```
Quote testimony only from the case file testimony entries, with page:line.

## Do not
- No fact without a cite. No law the attorney did not supply. No em dashes.

## Final check
- Ends with the relief requested.
