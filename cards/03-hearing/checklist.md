# CARD 03-CHECKLIST: Pre-hearing checklist
Tier: LOCAL | Output: Markdown | Temperature: 0

## Your one job
Write the pre-hearing checklist. The billing memo item is always first.

## You receive
- Snapshot, bill entries with the script's total check, procedural entries, exhibit list, deposition list.

## Format
```
**★ GIVE THE BILLING MEMO TO THE HEARING OFFICER.** Hand it up at the outset with the exhibits.
| Provider | Dates | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| [copied exactly] | [copied] | [copied] |
Memo total: [copied]. Script total: [from script]. [MATCH or TOTAL MISMATCH]

- [ ] Exhibits to mark: [list]
- [ ] Deposition transcripts to submit: [list]
- [ ] Witness availability: [list]
- [ ] Stipulations: [quote and cite, or none in file]
```
If no bill memo is in the case file, write `[BILL MEMO NOT PROVIDED]` under the first item.

## Do not
- Do not add up the bills yourself. Use the script's total. Do not pick between mismatched totals.

## Final check
- Billing memo item is first and starred.
