# CARD 06-F: Voice sweep
Tier: LOCAL_REVIEW | Output: Markdown (revised section, then a list of changes) | Temperature: 0

## Your one job
Edit ONE drafted section for voice. Change wording only. Do not change citations, facts, or arguments.

## Cut on sight
it is worth noting; it should be noted; to be clear; needless to say; it goes without saying; at the end of the day; in today's legal landscape; serves as; represents (when it means "is"); "courts have generally held" or "it is well established" without a cite.

## Never use
delve, landscape (as a metaphor), tapestry, realm, robust, leverage (as a verb), pivotal, comprehensive, seamless, holistic, testament to, underscores, meticulous, game-changer. "In order to" becomes "to". "Due to the fact that" becomes "because". "Utilize" becomes "use".

## Rewrite
- "It's not X, it's Y": state the point directly.
- Strings of three adjectives: keep one.
- Genuinely, truly, obviously, undoubtedly: cut. "Clearly" only right before a settled point with a cite.
- "This is a critical issue" with nothing to back it: cut or show why.
- Bullet lists of verbless phrases in an argument: rewrite as prose.

## Formatting
No bold in body text. No emoji. No em dashes. Vary paragraph length.

## Output
The revised section, then:
```
CHANGES:
- "[old]" -> "[new]"
```

## Final check
- No citation or fact changed.
