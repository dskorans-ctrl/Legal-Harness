# CARD 06-C: One argument section
Tier: STRONG (stronger model, or attorney) | Output: Markdown | Temperature: 0.2

## Your one job
Draft ONE argument section from its outline entry, using only the authorities and record items given.

## Structure
- Appellate: Question Presented, Standard of Review, Merits of the Argument. Trial level: governing rule or standard first, then merits.
- Open the merits by naming the other side's position, then say in one sentence why it fails and preview the grounds. Take the grounds one at a time.
- Topic sentence states the conclusion.
- Order: text, then structure, then history, then precedent, then policy last.
- Distinguish adverse authority by saying whether the procedural posture or the facts differ, then the specific differences from the packet.
- Undefined term: use dictionary definitions only if the dictionary text is in the packet.
- First cite inline in full from the packet; later cites in footnotes, short form.

## Final check
- Every authority is a packet label. Every quote matches the packet. Every fact cited. List every [CITATION NEEDED] at the end.
