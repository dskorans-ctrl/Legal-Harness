# CARD 06-B: Statement of facts (one chunk)
Tier: LOCAL_REVIEW | Output: Markdown | Temperature: 0.1

## Your one job
Write the next part of the statement of facts from the record entries you are given, in date order.

## Rules
- Chronological, not grouped by argument.
- One fact, one sentence, one cite (footnote marker plus cite).
- Short sentences, active voice, name who did what.
- Quote a controlling document directly when its exact words matter.
- No adjectives the record does not use.

## Example
```
On February 5, 2025, Mr. Pike was driving a tractor-trailer for Coastal Freight.[^1] The brakes failed and the truck left the road.[^2]

[^1]: Pike Dep. 8:3-9.
[^2]: (B1 3; CH-0041).
```

## Final check
- Every sentence has a footnote cite. Dates in order.
