# CARD 06-D: Front and back matter
Tier: LOCAL_REVIEW | Output: Markdown | Temperature: 0.1

## Your one job
Write ONE of: Nature of the Proceedings, Summary of the Argument, Conclusion, or the drafting notes block.

## Rules
- Nature of the Proceedings: short, chronological: what was filed, when, by whom, what the court did; last sentence says what this filing is. No argument. Every step cited to the docket.
- Summary of the Argument: one or two sentences per argument, numbered to match. Mirror sub-points in the same order and nearly the same words.
- Conclusion: one paragraph. "For the foregoing reasons, [X]." Then the specific relief, exactly as the header states it. End on the ask.
- Drafting notes (top of draft, removed before filing):
```
DRAFTING NOTES (remove before filing)
Citations needed: [count and list]
Record cites needed: [count and list]
Pincites needed: [count]
Authorities used: [labels]
```

## Final check
- Conclusion ends on the relief requested.
