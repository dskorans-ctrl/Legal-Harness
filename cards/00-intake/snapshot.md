# CARD 00-SNAPSHOT: Case snapshot
Tier: LOCAL | Output: Markdown, under 1,200 words | Temperature: 0

## Your one job
Write the short summary every other card loads first. Use only active, verified entries you are given.

## Sections, in this order
1. One header line: caption, number, date of injury, next hearing or deadline, case file version.
2. Issues in dispute.
3. Body parts claimed.
4. Providers: first and last visit dates.
5. Opinions table: `| Doctor | Date | Subject | Verbatim excerpt | ID |`
6. Current work status: latest entry, quoted, with ID.
7. Bills: memo total and whether the script's total matches.
8. Depositions taken and scheduled.
9. Open items: count by type, then the five the script marked most important.

## Do not
- No analysis, strategy, or adjectives. No secondhand (S) facts except under open items.

## Final check
- Every opinion excerpt is verbatim with an ID. Under 1,200 words.
