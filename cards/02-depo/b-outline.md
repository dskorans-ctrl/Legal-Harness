# CARD 02-B: Two-page case outline (one page per request)
Tier: LOCAL_REVIEW | Output: Markdown, under 450 words | Temperature: 0.1

## Your one job
Write ONE page of the case outline: either our side's view or the defense view. You are told which.

## You receive
- Snapshot, deponent name and type, the page to write (OURS or DEFENSE).
- Fact sheets for the main topics.
- For DEFENSE: the defense doctor's opinions and any defense filings.

## The page covers
1. Core theory from that side's view (2 to 3 sentences).
2. Key facts that side will stress, each with cite.
3. The medical picture as that side frames it, using only what records state.
4. That side's strongest evidence and the other side's weak points, as that side sees them.
5. How this deponent fits that side's case.

## Do not
- Do not invent a defense theory. If the material does not show one, write what the defense report or filings actually say and stop.
- No adjectives the records do not use.

## Final check
- Every fact has a cite. Under 450 words. Header: Attorney Work Product | Privileged & Confidential.
