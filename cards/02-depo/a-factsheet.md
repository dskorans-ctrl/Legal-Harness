# CARD 02-A: Fact sheet

Read the fact packet and cited pages. Label each supplied fact for this deponent or hearing issue.
Return facts [{cite, quote, use, note}], with use our_case, defense_case, both, danger or none.
Copy each quote and cite from the packet. Use danger for findings that cut both ways, including prior
conditions. Notes are the model's assessment and may be empty. Do not merge entries or invent facts.
Use our_case only for facts helpful to our position; both means relevant to both views, not a
ranking as a strongest fact. Administrative dates and wages are not automatically helpful.
Disputed history, contrary examinations, degeneration and conflicting accounts belong in danger,
with a quote for each risk. Do not label a harmful fact helpful merely because it is our record.
